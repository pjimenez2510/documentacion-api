We take security very seriously and try to patch each discovered security vulnerability as soon as possible. Also we use \href{https://dependabot.com/}{\texttt{automated tools}} to continually monitor vulnerable dependencies.\hypertarget{md__s_e_c_u_r_i_t_y_autotoc_md27}{}\doxysection{\texorpdfstring{Reporting a Vulnerability}{Reporting a Vulnerability}}\label{md__s_e_c_u_r_i_t_y_autotoc_md27}
We use \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_disclosure_(computer_security)}{\texttt{full disclosure}} for reporting vulnerabilities. Therefore please submit vulnerability reports through our \href{https://github.com/coma123/Spring-Boot-Blog-REST-API/issues/new?assignees=&labels=&template=bug_report.md&title=}{\texttt{issues section}}.
